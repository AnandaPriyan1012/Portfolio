# C18: show the reasoning inside AirKart

A fresh independent review of C17 found a useful storytelling opportunity in
AirKart's optional Lab. Its two working sliders produced an average waiting time
and a credit total, but did not show why those values changed. A small original
dispatch study now makes that existing explanation visible: separate arrivals,
individual waiting times, then one shared journey.

AirKart remains a team student-competition concept. The owner's existing notes
support the website and 3D-model contribution, but no original AirKart 3D asset
was supplied here. The new coded board is explicitly an illustration of the
teaching model, not supplied historical artwork, a live drone service, measured
performance or a flight simulation. The archive and its nested Lab remain
closed by default.

The upper immediate-versus-batch choice retains its separate invented 0/5-minute
and 8/5-credit example. Only the lower Lab drives the new study. For arrival rate
r orders per ten minutes and batch size n, the illustrated interval is 10/r;
order i arrives at i times that interval, and shared dispatch occurs at the last
arrival. Each planned wait is dispatch minus arrival. Their average matches the
existing (n−1)×(10/r)/2 result. The separate credit assumption remains a 12-credit
shared trip plus two credits per order, or 2+12/n per order. Model minutes refer
to batching time; transit and real operating constraints are excluded.

The full planned batch appears immediately. Native range inputs update the
complete static model without autoplay. An explicit Replay button runs one
compressed arrival sequence, followed by four-tenths of a second of order tokens
gathering into one group. The largest sequence finishes in roughly 2.6 seconds.
Stop shows the full model immediately; separate manual controls step through
arrivals and dispatch. The meaningful planned times remain readable throughout.
Status announcements describe deliberate steps, replay start and completion,
rather than narrating every automatic visual tick.

Bounded timers stop for disclosure closure, offscreen content, route unmount,
input changes, page visibility changes, opening screens and covering overlays.
The parent explicitly suppresses replay for its menu, project modal, AP OS and
tour states. Reduced motion, Motion Off, forced colours and detected data-saving
or low-power conditions retain manual steps and the static explanation. Opening
or returning to the Lab never restarts playback by itself. No new animation loop,
scroll controller, storage, dependency or media file was introduced.

The gold/glass board reuses the licensed pixel numeral font. An intrinsic
container query chooses two or up to four cards according to the actual Lab
width, rather than the whole viewport. This fixes an actual prototype overflow
at tablet widths. Both controls have a 44px minimum target. Tests cover narrow
phones, tablets, desktop, light mode, forced colours and motion alternatives.

Validation checks native controls and focus, all 36 rate/batch combinations,
per-order and aggregate calculations, finite replay, manual takeover and
cancellation. Existing upper-choice and lower-slider regressions remain separate
from the new study. One independent mean assertion initially rounded a floating
sum below an exact half; the preserved test failure was resolved with exact
rational arithmetic, without changing the production calculation.

Native hidden-document execution is capability-gated: this managed headless
browser keeps the original document visible after opening and selecting another
tab. That check is explicitly skipped when the actual visibility signal cannot
be produced; the handler is reviewed, not represented as a browser pass. The
exact local/public results, build identity, screenshots and deployment evidence
are recorded separately under `/workspace/cycle-eighteen-release-review`.

The exact AP asset, genuine website imagery, approved original films and frame
derivatives, project credits, recognition qualifications and previous page
features are preserved. The prior Library materialization HTTP 401 was not
retried. Inaccessible certificate originals and unmatched MUN material remain
unpublished, and sharing permissions were not changed.
