# C22: keep reading through the real AccuFlow captures

A fresh live review found a reading gap in AccuFlow's existing four-view
exploration. At the foot of the 390px Product capture, its top choices were
about 1,000px above the reader in both Work and the project notes. The original
phone image is tall, and native scrolling works. This change adds a deliberate
next step where reading finishes rather than shortening or cropping the work.

Work's route deck, its optional interface drawer and the AccuFlow case study
now have Previous capture and Next capture controls beneath their existing
content. They follow Home, Product, How it works and the initial guide
interface, with the appropriate original labels in each context. The first and
last controls are disabled at their boundaries; there is no automatic wrap.
The small counter explicitly describes four captured views, not all six pages
in the public prototype or visitor progress. Business and About remain separate
source links.

A footer selection changes the genuine source and focuses its existing top
choice, bringing that reading start below the navigation. The one-time handoff
runs after the committed image ratio establishes the new layout. Work reuses
the existing measured scroll coordinator and refreshes its scene geometry;
case notes use their existing native tab scroll margin. No second scroll owner,
URL entry, animation loop or new storage key was added. Top choices and native
tab keyboard interaction retain their ordinary behavior. Hover, passive
scrolling, image decoding, errors and resizing do not request another handoff
or take focus from a reader who has moved on.

The shared navigation sits outside the Atlas's keyed panel, keeping its
controls mounted while the selected content changes. Existing independent Work
and case choices, case-return reading position, source links, larger-image
inspection and image-error alternatives remain. The Homepage phone view still
uses its accurately identified desktop-format source. The default Work guide
view and all original sources, captions, dates and prototype qualifications
are unchanged.

The footer uses the established dark glass and pale-gold material, with the
licensed pixel type confined to its counter. Its native buttons are at least
58px high; focus indication, forced-system colours, native touch and quiet
motion variants remain available. It introduces no ambient animation and no
new asset, dependency or font.

Verification covers all three capture surfaces at desktop, phone and narrow
widths, forward and reverse selection, boundaries, selected-control clearance,
source matching, passive input, delayed and failed images, focus continuity,
saved choices and case returns. Independent visual checks include light theme,
forced colours, coarse input, reduced motion and motion off. Exact compiled
and public evidence is recorded in `/workspace/cycle-twenty-two-release-review`.
The adjacent regression run initially found three outdated assertions for
Vallalar copy already replaced in C20. Those two assertions now check the
verified service and limited-reach narrative; no production fix was needed.
An independent first-run enabled/disabled matcher mistake is also retained
separately from the corrected visual result.

The exact AP logo, global scroll coordinator, C21 image-detail inspection,
films, authentic frame derivatives and recognition qualifications remain. The
